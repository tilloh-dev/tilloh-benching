Write a short essay in Markdown on the question: "Should small teams run language models locally?"

The readers are technical leads of software teams with 3 to 20 people who are deciding between running open-weight models on their own hardware and using hosted model APIs.

Requirements:
- Length: 400–700 words in total.
- Start with a title as a level-1 heading, followed by 3–5 sections, each with its own level-2 heading.
- Discuss the trade-offs in a balanced way. Cover at least cost, privacy, latency, output quality and maintenance effort, and say for each where local models win and where hosted APIs win.
- Include at least one concrete example, such as a specific team, workload or hardware setup with rough numbers.
- End with a clear recommendation: say what a typical small team should do and under which conditions the answer changes.
- Write in clear, plain English prose. Use lists sparingly.
